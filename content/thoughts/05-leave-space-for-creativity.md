---
title: Leave Space for Creativity
kind: thought
---
Low level actions and low effort actions should be automated or written down or at best should not need the brain to always remember to do them. This is usually the first step to setting up systems and structures.

The brain isn’t entirely being utilized efficiently when it has to figure out from scratch a problem it has solved before. The solution is systems.

You fix a bug, you don’t document how you fixed it or even setup systems to help you catch it faster, you’re not utilizing your brain efficiently.

Powerful companies and productive people prioritize documentation over anything. Never let anything live inside your head. Leave space for creativity.
