---
title: Never Party on a Sunday
kind: poem
---
I seemed to zone out, too lazy to focus.
I feel a little bit uneasy, lost in my thoughts.
My head's aching, my stomach's turning, my feet's wobbly.
I sit up in bed with a splitting headache.
My curtains' drawn, I don't dare open them.
My whole body feels like it's been trampled by a herd of cows.
Suddenly, I feel it, that jerking feeling in the pit of my stomach, the uneasiness.
I rush to the bathroom to eject the contents of my stomach.
Seemingly, refreshed but late for work, my eyes diverted to the wall clock.
Alas, I am late for work so I grab a few bites of bread.
I dress up, put on a pair of sunglasses and step out of the three- bedroom flat I reside in.
This feeling of hangover will be a constant reminder never to party on a Sunday.
